# Paper VIII Evidence Boundary

Paper VIII is a static object-language paper. Its category and functoriality
results are proved from declared finite-dimensional SOF data and do not depend
on a paper-local computational experiment.

The representation, geometry, Markov, graph, activation, and external-sector
examples in the manuscript are illustrative realization classes. They are not
imported numerical evidence and do not make Paper VIII the owner of those
applications.

Later application scripts may instantiate the Paper VIII interface, but their
scientific findings remain with the paper that owns the corresponding static
audit, deformation, Registry record, report, or comparison.
