# Candidate scope

## Included mathematical results

1. Complete-event cyclic realization and quadratic representatives, supplied
   as execution foundations.
2. Exact delayed-chain costs with actual endpoints and an attained symbolic
   extraction maximum `K_n`.
3. Upper cursor replay, lower erasure-shadow fusion elimination, and the
   exact amplitude of the entire actual one-block observation fibre.
4. Necessary extremal structure, preserving the original extraction order.
5. The proved reduction of the global comparison to multiblock fibres.
6. Moment-sensitive terminal routing for arbitrary named upper packet cycles,
   and identification of the physical gap moment with the retained forest.
7. A joint within-root / cross-root / gap-moment defect and phase-aware winding
   payment, giving partition-relative singleton replay across all terminal ranks.
8. The exact maximum over all all-upper observations, allowing whole-packet
   transfers, lower prefusions and arbitrary legal interleavings.
9. Localization of any global excess to multiblock observations with at least
   one lower-layer survivor.

The arbitrary terminal route assumes an occupied source upper port 0;
the actual reserved replay supplies this condition via its surviving cursor
anchor. Its common cut is a proof reindexing, not a free physical rotation.
The final rotation is paid. The adapter retains the supplied forest and
reservation indices without assuming that transfers precede fusions.

The full singleton forest budget now pays the terminal moment, event cursor
and same-partition minimum together, without a comb-shape or transfer-first
hypothesis. The packed extension constructs one same-observation word and
uses lower fusion elimination on the same actual lower log. Its terminal
route uses physical rank `r`, independently of the number `t` of transferred
packets. A packet order is not converted into an atom permutation.

The exact singleton and general all-upper maxima are over the union of all
terminal ranks, not every fixed rank or every partition. The residual global
domain is a class of observations, not a class of unordered partitions alone.

## Excluded from this candidate

General lower-surviving multiblock domination, Josephus-order global optimality,
the full history/literal Pareto frontier and tight partition-diameter
asymptotics remain separate problems. Retention interval optimization,
matching shared loading and nested matching hybrid loading are not included as additional
machine-checked results. Their source-level developments remain in the
original research checkout, not as premises or runtime dependencies here.

## Evidence

The paper supplies mathematical proofs. The Lean kernel checks the selected
declarations and their transitive dependencies. Validator tests check the
declared byte, import, reader and audit interfaces. A local replay receipt
does not certify the Python executor against Lean or establish an independent
review. No new large finite census is necessary for this closure.

Published Paper XLV/XLVI content and the earlier local single-block index
are not rewritten. The latter is copied unchanged into `provenance/`, and
the new provenance record verifies its 149 Lean files against the active
mirror. The current source paths listed there describe where bytes were
copied from; the build resolves only within this package and locked Git
dependencies.

unchanged. The same repair was applied to the development owner, and the
copy provenance records it. Earlier receipts are not rebound to those bytes.
