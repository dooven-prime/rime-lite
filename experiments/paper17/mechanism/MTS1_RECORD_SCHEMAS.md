## Source-transition cache

The source cache contains exactly 459 records: one for each of 153 registered
sources at current times `1`, `2`, and `3`. A record binds the exact
microscopic state, raw signed drive, clipped drive, touched-target clipping
fates, three exact coarse projections, the Paper XVII replay gates, and the
closed source-local descriptor list.

The touched-target universe consists of targets receiving at least one
pre-summation edge contribution from the current nonzero source support. Fate
boundaries are exact:

```text
LOWER:    u <= 0
INTERIOR: 0 < u < 1
UPPER:    u >= 1
```

Targets with exact cancellation to zero remain touched and are classified as
`LOWER`.

## Pair-transition records

The pair surface contains exactly 6,975 records: all 2,325 registered pairs on
the common transition grid `1->2`, `2->3`, and `3->4`. Every record references
two completed source-cache records. It may not run the operator again.

The record stores exact state/raw-drive geometry, exact coarse residuals, and
the complete oriented `3 x 3` clipping-fate table. Pair orientation is frozen
by the scoped amendment:

```text
source_a = min(source_id_1, source_id_2)
source_b = max(source_id_1, source_id_2)
delta    = value(source_a) - value(source_b)
```

The schema validator checks `r_corr = r_clip - r_raw`, the residual quadrant,
observation-equality flags, and `r_clip = 5 * delta_z_next` whenever current
observations agree.

## Storage policy

Final records and payloads use portable result-root-relative paths. Absolute
paths, parent traversal, backslashes, and `.runtime-work` paths are forbidden.
Large exact payloads remain sidecars bound by byte length, codec identifier,
dimension, entry count, and SHA-256. Cache metadata is written before pair
metadata. Pair JSON Lines are sharded deterministically by cohort and
transition. A final inventory must bind all ordered records and payloads.

The microscopic-state codec is implemented and frozen. Separate domain-tagged
codecs for raw drive, clipped drive, and clipping fate remain producer gates;
the schema names them but does not silently treat the microscopic-state codec
as interchangeable.
