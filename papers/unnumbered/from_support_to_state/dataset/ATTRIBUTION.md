The two carrier files in `data/carrier/` are derived from the MaleCNS v1.0
connectome data. The source dataset is a collaboration of HHMI Janelia FlyEM,
the University of Cambridge Department of Zoology, the MRC Laboratory of
Molecular Biology, and Google Research. The official source is
<https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/>. It declares the MaleCNS dataset
under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0):
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.

The exact original input URLs, sizes, and SHA-256 values are recorded in the
included published Paper XVI `experiments/paper16/upstream-provenance.v1.json`. The
derived carrier's exact byte identities are in this package's `manifest.json`
and result JSON. Redistribution of these derived files does not imply that
the original collaborators endorse this analysis or its model assumptions.
