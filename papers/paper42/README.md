# Paper XLII: Registered Proper-Arc Summaries

This is the numbered, version 1.0 reader edition of *Registered Proper-Arc
Chains and Placement Fibers in a Circular Collapse System*. The manuscript,
paper-local bibliography, and reader PDF are candidate publication files.
No Paper XLII DOI or publication anchor is claimed yet.

The historical manuscript and HAL-targeted PDF remain under
`papers/unnumbered/proper_arc_registered_summaries/`. The identifier
`hal-05848115v1` records the earlier submission, not an assertion that HAL
accepted or published it. The fixed finite controls and the separately
published [Lean companion](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23941878) retain
their original proof ownership. Their exact identities are recorded in
`experiments/paper42/evidence-map.v1.json`.

Build this edition from the repository root:

```text
python -B papers/tex/build.py --output-dir output/tex/paper42 paper42
```

Paper XLI's fixed-pair result is cited by its published DOI
[10.5281/zenodo.24137725](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24137725).
The all-parameter proper-arc classification is proved in this manuscript;
the finite controls are bounded checks. The Lean companion formalizes the
origin, chain, placement-fibre, summary, and exact-count layer, not the
fixed-pair specialization or Section 8's generating functions and asymptotics.
