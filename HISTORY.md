claim-boundary changes. It intentionally omits day-by-day editing notes,
repeated build output, and internal planning.

their declared artifacts remain the authority for mathematical and
computational claims.

## 2026-07-27--28: Papers IV--VII Claim Corrections

### Paper IV

Paper IV now separates four layers:

1. general fixed finite-arrangement theorems;
2. an exact census for the declared rational nine-point arrangement;
3. complex128 Rubik registration against that arrangement;
4. a conditional exact Rubik corollary.

Numerical registration does not prove exact QT/HT commutation or exact
rational joint spectral data.

### Paper V

Paper V was reframed as a static separation theorem for direct support,
projected products, full words, commutator support, and Lie depth. The former
global repair-calculus interpretation and Type I--IV taxonomy were retired.
The value `999` means cutoff-unreached, not exact infinity.

### Paper VI

Paper VI replaced the first-version nonnormal fragmentation narrative with:

- a full-matrix commutator Jacobian certificate;
- a combined commutativity/normality derivative certificate;
- normality-gated pointwise sector registrations.

These records are pointwise computational certificates, not a nonlinear
spectral atlas, moving-wall theorem, or positive dynamic SOF instance.

### Paper VII

Paper VII was rebuilt around image--kernel incidence, fixed-rank geometry,
rectangular rank protection, and promotion limits. The former Generic
Completion Principle was withdrawn. Its finite Lie atlas is a computational
observation, not a theorem that low-order support determines depth.

## 2026-07-26: Papers I--III Unbundling and Correction

Papers I, II, and III were released independently.

### Paper I

- The six-layer Rubik spectrum is a registered block census.
- The `cp` and `ep` reductions are analytic.
- The `co` accidental degeneracy remains computational.
- The `eo` assignment remains a numerical-representation observation.
- Partition integrality is a valid sufficient certificate format, but the
  tested canonical face partition fails its integer hypothesis and does not
  prove canonical Rubik rationality.

### Paper II

- The nine QT/HT sectors and ten-edge graph retain computational status.
- The noncommutative block-support test is a localizer, not a sufficient edge
  criterion: 15 candidate overlaps comprise nine Type I labelled edges and six
  nonedges.
- Type I/II are post-certification labels rather than universal mechanisms.

### Paper III

The first-version T7 interpretation was withdrawn. The revised paper proves
the image--kernel criterion and separates support-graph paths from projected
matrix composition. The five canonical Rubik paths are graph-only obstruction
records; all routed products are machine-zero with a structural
block-preservation explanation.

The invalid historical ORCID `0009-0000-8247-2898` was retired. The canonical
author ORCID is `0009-0007-9561-2892`.
